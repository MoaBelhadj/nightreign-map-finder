Live companion

Share your game window and get short tips while you play. Your HUD bars are read on this device, many times a second. Nothing is recorded or saved, and Claude only sees a frame when you ask.

Input

Your browser asks before anything is shared. Pick the Nightreign window, not the whole screen, so the overlay is never part of what gets read.

Live capture runs in the local copy of this page. claude.ai doesn't let pages share your screen or open the pop-out. Screenshots and questions still work here.

  1. Open your nightreign-map-finder folder (or get it from GitHub) and double-click start-live.cmd. It needs Python.
  2. Edge opens the Live companion. If it doesn't, open the local copy while start-live.cmd is running.
  3. Press Share game window and pick ELDEN RING NIGHTREIGN.

Controls

Hotkeys work while this page or the pop-out has focus: P pause, M mute, 1 auto, 2 combat, 3 exploration, N next stop (Shift+N back), D next phase, Q ask about now, V voice, O pop-out, S spoilers. While the game has focus, use voice instead.

Readings
Overlay and performance

Opacity and floating placement apply inside this page. The pop-out is a separate always-on-top window: drag it where you want it. The browser keeps it opaque.

Calibrate HUD

With the HUD showing and full HP, press Find bars. Boxes appear on the preview. To fix one, pick it below and drag a box around the full bar.
